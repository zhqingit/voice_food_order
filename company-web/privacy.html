Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Resto AI LLC ("RestoAI", "we", "our", "us") operates the VoxEats mobile application, the VoxEats phone ordering lines of participating restaurants, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and phone number you provide when you create an account.
  • Order information: Restaurant choices, menu items, pickup or delivery details, and payment status.
  • Phone orders: When you call a restaurant's VoxEats line, we receive your caller ID phone number and the details of your order.
  • Voice data: When you order by voice in the app or by phone, your audio is streamed to our servers and to our speech and language providers for real-time transcription and understanding.
  • Payment information: Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Device information: Basic technical data such as app version, device model, and operating system version, used for troubleshooting and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To accept, process, and fulfill your food orders.
  • To turn your speech into menu actions during voice ordering.
  • To communicate with you about your orders and account, including by text message (see Section 4).
  • To improve our services, including diagnosing errors.

3. Microphone and Audio

The app asks for microphone permission only for voice ordering. Audio from the app and from phone calls is processed in real time to take your order and is not kept after the ordering session, unless required for safety, legal, or troubleshooting reasons. We do not use your audio for advertising and do not sell it.

4. Text Messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number when you place an order by phone or in the app, we may send you text messages about that order, such as order changes, payment links, and pickup or delivery updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to run VoxEats:

  • Google Cloud (including Vertex AI, Speech-to-Text, and Text-to-Speech): voice recognition, AI language processing, and voice responses.
  • Twilio: phone calls and text messages.
  • Stripe: payment processing.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies, and we share only what is needed to provide the service. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your account data and order history are stored on our servers. We use encryption in transit (TLS/SSL), access controls, and other industry-standard measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

7. Data Retention

Account and order data is retained while your account is active. Voice audio is processed in transit and is not stored long-term. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, including text messages (reply STOP).

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will point out significant changes in the app or on this page.

11. Contact Us

Resto AI LLC. If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact us at restoai@rest-oai.com.
