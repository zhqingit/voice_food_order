SMS Terms

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Program

VoxEats Order Notifications, operated by Resto AI LLC.

What You Will Receive

Text messages about orders you place with participating restaurants through VoxEats, by phone or in the VoxEats app: order changes, payment links, and pickup or delivery status. We do not send marketing messages.

How You Opt In

You opt in by giving your mobile number when you place an order by phone or in the VoxEats app and agreeing to receive text messages about that order.

Message Frequency and Cost

Message frequency varies, usually 1 to 5 messages per order. Message and data rates may apply.

How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. You will receive one text confirming that you have opted out, and no further messages after that.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email us at restoai@rest-oai.com.

Other Terms

  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.
