VoxEats Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

VoxEats ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered voice food ordering service operated by RestoAI. This page describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, and phone number you provide at sign-up.
  • Order information — restaurant choices, menu items, delivery/pickup details, and payment status (we do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by third-party processors).
  • Voice recordings — when you use voice ordering, your microphone audio is captured and streamed to our servers and to our speech and language providers (Google Cloud) for real-time transcription and understanding.
  • Device information — basic, non-identifying technical data such as app version, device model, and OS version, used for debugging and reliability.

How we use information

  • To accept and fulfill your orders.
  • To convert your speech into menu actions during voice ordering.
  • To communicate with you about your order or account.
  • To improve the service, including diagnosing errors.

Microphone and audio

The app requests microphone permission solely for voice ordering. Audio is streamed in real time, processed to determine your order, and is not retained after the ordering session unless required for safety, legal, or troubleshooting reasons. We do not use your audio for advertising and do not sell it.

Third parties

We use Google Cloud (including Vertex AI, Speech-to-Text, and Text-to-Speech) to process voice input and generate responses. Voice and order data sent to these providers is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We retain account and order data for as long as your account is active. Voice audio is processed in transit and is not stored long-term. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact below).

Children

VoxEats is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including HTTPS in transit and access controls, to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or on this page. Continued use of VoxEats after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
